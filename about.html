Bio

Christina Cappelli is a Brooklyn-based multidisciplinary designer and creative technologist whose speculative explorations of natural systems drive immersive computational videos, interactive programming, and generative design. She’s a National Science Foundation and NYU Entrepreneurial Institute grantee who’s also photographed international campaigns and produced development and casting for Lifetime, the Travel Channel, and MTV.
