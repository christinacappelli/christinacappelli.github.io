About

Hi there! I'm a Brooklyn-based artist and creative technologist working at the intersection of science, design, and immersive storytelling.

My work explores obscured architectures of life and investigates how unseen systems shape perception through research-driven, speculative approaches. Using generative processes, computation, and mixed media, I focus on patterns we tend to overlook—those buried in scale, hidden in plain sight, or unfamiliar by nature.

With a background in media production, photography, and technology, I approach storytelling through both visual fluency and systems thinking. My tools range from JavaScript, TouchDesigner, and generative AI to hands-on methods like material exploration, rapid prototyping, and mixed media construction. By taking an interdisciplinary approach, I turn abstract research into something people can move through, sense, and interpret for themselves—bridging technical concepts with lived experience.

My work has been supported by the National Science Foundation, NYU’s Entrepreneurial Institute, and the NYC Future Manufacturing Collective, and presented through platforms at the Berggruen Institute, Genspace, and New York University. Alongside my studio practice, I’ve worked as a photographer on international campaigns across England, Ireland, and Spain, and produced content for television networks like Lifetime, Travel Channel, and MTV.

I hold a BFA in Performing Arts and an MPS in Interactive Telecommunications from NYU’s Tisch School of the Arts. Whether through interactive installations, computational media, or speculative ecosystems, I aim to build experiences that reframe perception, invite inquiry, and bridge science with creative engagement.
